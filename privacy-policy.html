Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 14, 2026

Welcome to CamPlus. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your personal information is collected, used, and disclosed when you use our mobile application (the "App").

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. We designed this policy to be compliant with Google Play Store requirements.

1. Information We Collect

a. Account and Profile Data

When you register for an account, we may collect minimal personal information such as your name, email address, and profile picture. We use Supabase to securely manage your authentication and backend database needs.

b. User-Generated Content (Camera & Photos)

Our App may request access to your device's camera and photo library to allow you to upload profile pictures or share images within the App. Images are uploaded to our secure servers (via Supabase) only when you explicitly choose to share them. We do not access your camera or photos without your direct action and permission.

c. Usage Data & Analytics

To improve your experience, we collect anonymized diagnostic and usage data. We use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Analytics & Mixpanel: Used to understand how you interact with the App (e.g., screens visited, buttons clicked) so we can improve our features.
  • Sentry: Used to track app crashes and errors. If the App crashes, a report containing device information (such as OS version, device model, and state of the App at the time of the crash) is automatically sent to help us fix the issue.

d. Push Notifications

We use Firebase Cloud Messaging to send you important updates, alerts, and transactional messages. You can opt-out of these notifications at any time through your device's system settings.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for various purposes, including:

  • To provide and maintain the App's core functionality
  • To manage your account securely via Supabase
  • To notify you about changes to our App or services
  • To provide customer support
  • To monitor usage metrics and fix bugs in real-time
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues

3. Third-Party Services and Data Processors

We do not sell your data. We may share your information only with secure third-party vendors that help us operate the App. Below are the third-party service providers used by the App, along with links to their privacy policies:

  • Supabase (Database & Authentication)
  • Google Analytics for Firebase (Analytics)
  • Mixpanel (Product Analytics)
  • Sentry (Crash Reporting)

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your personal data only for as long as is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy. You have the right to request the deletion of your account and its associated data.

To request data deletion: You can delete your account directly from within the App settings, or you can contact us to request the complete deletion of your personal information from our databases (Supabase).

5. Security of Data

The security of your data is important to us. Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest by our backend provider (Supabase). However, remember that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your personal data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

6. Children's Privacy

Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with Personal Data, please contact us.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy.
