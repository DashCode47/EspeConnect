Request Data Deletion

At CamPlus, we take your privacy seriously. If you wish to delete your account and all associated data from our servers, you can easily do so.

Option 1: In-App Deletion (Recommended)

The fastest way to delete your data is directly within the CamPlus app:

  1. Open the CamPlus app and log in.
  2. Go to your Profile or Settings.
  3. Look for the option "Delete Account" and follow the prompts.

* This action will immediately and permanently delete your data from our servers (powered by Supabase).

Option 2: Email Deletion Request

If you cannot access the app, you can request manual data deletion by sending us an email from the email address associated with your account.

Please click the button below or email us at daviddev47@hotmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request".
